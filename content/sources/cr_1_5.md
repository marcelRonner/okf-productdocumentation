# CR-1.5 – Partner Proposals During Withdrawal

A functional change to the cash withdrawal in the ATM system.

---

## Request

Requested by the owner on 2026-10-30: *"Show on the ATM Screen proposals what i could do with the
money i take out."*

## Analysis

The ATM shows the Customer what they could do with the cash they are withdrawing. In v1.4 the domain
model has no concept for such a suggestion or for the merchant behind it, so it gains two entities.
"Screen" is not a domain term and stays lowercase; the ATM System actor already controls the screen
as a local peripheral. The change touches the domain model, the business process, the Withdraw Cash
use case and its activity diagram, the ATM System actor and the Withdraw Cash epic. The Transaction
lifecycle does not change.

## Decisions

- **Content** — a Proposal is a commercial offer from a Partner.
- **Timing** — Proposals are displayed after the ATM ejects the Card and until the Customer retrieves
  it, between steps 4a.6 and dispensing.
- **Selection** — the ATM selects from the Proposals it holds locally, by the withdrawal amount only.
  No Customer or Account data is used.
- **No matching Proposal** — if none matches or the local selection fails, nothing is displayed and
  the withdrawal continues unaffected.
- **Display only** — the Customer cannot accept, print or otherwise act on a Proposal.
- **Recording** — the ATM writes an AuditLog entry for each display.
- **Modelling** — `Proposal` and `Partner` are new entities. `Bank` cooperates with `Partner`,
  `Partner` offers `Proposal`, `ATM` holds `Proposal`.
- **Release** — the new story belongs to a new row, *R4 – Delight*.
- **Card prompt stays dominant.** CR-1.1 ejects the Card first so that the Customer does not forget
  it, so a Proposal must not displace the prompt to retrieve the Card.
- **Failed dispensing accepted.** A Proposal appears before the cash is confirmed as dispensed, so a
  Customer can see one and then receive no cash. Because Proposals are display only, nothing can be
  taken up, and the existing dispensing error applies.
- **Assumption:** how Proposals reach each ATM is out of scope. The change assumes every ATM holds a
  current set; distributing them needs its own change request.
- **Assumption:** the ATM displays at most three matching Proposals at once.
- **Assumption:** a Proposal matches when the withdrawal amount lies between its `minAmount` and
  `maxAmount` and the current date lies between its `validFrom` and `validUntil`.
- **Assumption:** the Proposal and Partner attributes in the domain model are sufficient; a Partner
  contract or payment is not modelled.
- **Assumption:** displaying commercial offers during a withdrawal is permitted by the Bank's policies
  and advertising rules; the Bank confirms.

## Acceptance

- While waiting to retrieve the Card, a Customer whose withdrawal amount matches a Proposal sees it,
  and the ATM writes an AuditLog entry.
- Without a matching Proposal the withdrawal runs as in v1.4.
- The prompt to retrieve the Card stays visible, and the Customer cannot act on a Proposal.
