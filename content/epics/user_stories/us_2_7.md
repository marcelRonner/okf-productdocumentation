# US-2.7 – See Partner Proposals While Waiting for Card

## Card

As a **Customer**, I **WOULD LIKE** to see Partner Proposals for my withdrawal amount while I wait to retrieve my Card so that I get ideas for what I could do with the cash.

## Conversation

- After ejecting the Card, the ATM selects from the Proposals it holds locally, using the withdrawal amount only. No Customer or Account data is used.
- A Proposal matches when the withdrawal amount lies between its `minAmount` and `maxAmount` and the current date lies between its `validFrom` and `validUntil`. The ATM displays at most three.
- Proposals are display only, and the prompt to retrieve the Card stays visible.
- Corresponds to activity diagram step in [Withdraw Cash](../../use_case/withdraw_cash/withdrawCash.md): "ATM displays Proposals" (between Card ejection and cash dispensing).[^withdraw-cash]
- KANO: **Attractive** — a pleasant surprise, and its absence disappoints nobody.

## Confirmation

- **Given** a Card has been ejected and the ATM holds a matching Proposal
  **When** the ATM waits for the Customer to retrieve the Card
  **Then** the ATM displays the Proposal alongside the prompt to retrieve the Card and writes an AuditLog entry.

- **Given** more than three Proposals match
  **When** they are displayed
  **Then** the ATM shows at most three.

- **Given** no Proposal matches or the selection fails
  **When** the Card is ejected
  **Then** no Proposal is displayed, no AuditLog entry is written for one, and the withdrawal continues unchanged.

- **Given** Proposals are displayed
  **When** the Customer retrieves the Card
  **Then** the ATM dispenses cash and the Proposals disappear.
